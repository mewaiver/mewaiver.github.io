- Branching and merging.
- Integrity and data verification.
- Performance and repository operations.

Select relevant mechanisms and explain them accurately.

Do not exaggerate or oversimplify Git's architectural innovations.

### G. Lasting Influence

Distinguish lasting technical lessons from historical circumstances that cannot necessarily be replicated.

Avoid generic conclusions about innovation, passion, perseverance, or changing the world.
