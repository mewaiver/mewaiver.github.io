## Audit limits and decision principles

- The score is subordinate to factual or ethical concerns. CANIS, modular-monolith and thread-dump articles are unpublished pending checks; the untraceable AI Cloud Code story is removed.
- Generic articles in the same domain were not automatically merged. PostgreSQL/MySQL, Redis/Memcached and MongoDB/Couchbase address different decisions and remain separate.
- Drafts are included in the original collection, but excluded from the published count. No date was changed to imply freshness.
- Target publication type denotes a feasible future format, not present compliance with it or peer review.
- Original author metadata is `Tech Chain Editorial` throughout this collection; first-person and project articles require author verification. No external primary source was found within most older article bodies.
- Public category slugs remain as implemented. Suggested future categories: software-engineering, software-architecture, distributed-systems, computing-infrastructure, artificial-intelligence, cybersecurity, engineering-practice. Map existing categories only after a URL and navigation review.

## Repository and publication mechanics

- All original articles were Markdown with YAML front matter in `app/content/posts/`; filename minus the final `.md` is the route slug. The original 80 included 78 published articles and two drafts. Front matter uses `title`, `date`, `category`, `author`, `excerpt`, optional `image`, `draft`, `updated`, `type` and optional extended publication metadata. Exact original metadata, tags, section headings, outbound links and source hashes are recorded in `EDITORIAL_AUDIT.json`.
- The original category slugs were `engineering`, `security`, `cloud-computing`, `databases`, `iot`, `blockchain`, `sustainability`, `digital-commerce` and `ai`. No retained original article had a nonempty `tags` array. The former `digital-commerce` and `sustainability` categories are now empty; only categories with published articles are statically generated.
- `app/services/posts.js` reads the collection; `/posts/[slug]` supplies article routes and adjacent-article navigation; `/artigos` supplies title/summary/category/author search and category filtering. Homepage selection uses the most recent published article unless `featured` is set. `app/scripts/generate-sitemap.js` now lists published posts and nonempty published categories. The source collection has no RSS/feed generator, and no previous redirect mechanism. Six semantic replacements use static HTML redirect fallbacks; GitHub Pages cannot issue HTTP 301 from this repository.
- Original cross-article links appeared mainly as “related reading” and “next” lines. These were inspected after deletion: nine links were retargeted to a semantic canonical and 36 lines to deleted or unpublished articles were removed. The content validator checks published-target links and redirect chains.
- `docs/editorial/` is outside the Next.js `app` and `public` trees; its audit, backlog and archive files are operational records, not public posts. The exported site and sitemap were checked for accidental inclusion.
